## 1. Invariant feasibility

- **Any implementation, mechanically exposed:** **Achievable with a caveat.** Serving can be independent of the source transport, but the source’s cursors, outcomes, and values must survive the channel codec. An implementation with host objects in those values cannot be exposed under the proposed portable profile. The spec already states that refusal condition, so its unqualified “any” claim is too broad. [serve.md:191](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:191) [serve.md:256](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:256)
- **WebSocket or UDP:** **Achievable with a caveat.** WebSocket can carry a portable source through an established connection. UDP’s proposed 1200-byte frame limit cannot carry every source outcome; the spec itself requires fragmentation before claiming content service over UDP. Thus universal UDP exposure is **not achieved by v1 as written**. Its claim that WebSocket already specifies a CBOR profile also conflicts with the current Transit default. - **P2P NAT traversal:** **Achievable only conditionally.** For UDP, full-cone mappings commonly permit direct reachability; restricted and port-restricted mappings generally need coordinated, same-socket punching, and success still depends on both networks’ mapping and filtering. Symmetric mapping, some CGNAT combinations, and UDP-blocking networks can defeat direct UDP. CGNAT alone does not determine the outcome. For WebSocket, direct connection needs a reachable TCP acceptor; the proposed UDP punch does not make a WebSocket acceptor reachable. Two browser peers cannot accept WebSocket connections, so they need a reachable third peer’s relay under this design. If outbound access to that peer is blocked too, neither transport can provide a route. - **No server, client, or privileged node:** **Achievable at the serve-protocol level, with an honest qualification.** Dialer and acceptor are establishment roles; either peer can serve sessions once connected. A meeting peer is an operational relay and availability dependency for peers that cannot connect directly. It is a *server in that conventional network sense*, although the protocol gives it no exclusive authority or privileged node type. Tell the owner plainly: universal **direct** P2P and universal NAT traversal are technically impossible; conditional direct paths plus a reachable, voluntarily run relay preserve peer symmetry, but cannot remove the relay’s availability role. ## 2. Toy example walkthrough

An interpreter puts the string in a local stream whose handle supplies the needed reader surface, registers that handle and identity in its serve table, and publishes a serve descriptor with a reachable WebSocket channel candidate. The remote interpreter attaches, opens a session by identity, receives the declared surface and source-minted anchors, then calls `next` on its proxy. A cache miss records demand; `proxy-step` sends a cursor-and-budget request; `serve-step` calls the source handle; the response carries its outcome and successor cursor unchanged. The proxy returns the string as ordinary stream data. Later source eviction produces the source’s `gap` and recovery cursor; channel loss instead detaches the proxy, and a fresh attachment can use the kept cursor. Nothing in that operation path branches on “string.” [serve.md:193](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:193) [serve.md:224](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:224) [serve.md:328](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:328) [serve.md:369](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:369) [serve.md:420](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:420) [serve.md:647](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.serve.md:647) [UCF.md:946](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/yin.vm.universal-continuation-format.md:946)

**What does not yet work:** this is a design target, while the current WebSocket handle has no reader surface and the current serving composition forwards a copy. The new serve/proxy composition must be implemented. Also, the proposed proxy says it installs a terminating `blocked` outcome in its cache but does not specify when that cached answer is invalidated and demand reissued; without that rule, a read that initially blocks can stay blocked after the string becomes available. ## 5. Verdict

**NOT READY.** The spec needs the writer-only anchor fix, cached-`blocked` retry rule, and an honest UDP portability boundary before implementation. Section 16’s contract and companion-document edits are also prerequisites. The owner must be told that direct P2P through every NAT and network is technically impossible; reachable peer relays make many otherwise blocked cases work, but they are real routing dependencies.
