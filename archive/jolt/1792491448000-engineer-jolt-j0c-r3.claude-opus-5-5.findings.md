### The E1 harness (item 2)

**Strict checks:**
- every field of an ok packet;
- for error packets, every field except `:yang.cst/errors` (outcome, version, unit, grammar, complete);
- `cst/first-failure`;
- every probe answer and its `first-failure` call sequence.

**Every jolt packet passes `(packet/validate! p {:source source})`.**

**Error packets also require:**
- at least one error;
- the first error's `:kind` and `:span` equal to the JVM's;
- the first error's `:message` is a non-empty string;
- the first error's `:line`/`:column` equal to the JVM's when the offending token is real. When it is helper-fabricated, they must equal the pinned constant.
  - Helper-fabricated means INDENT, DEDENT, EOF, or a zero-width NEWLINE. This is decided from jolt's own record of the offending token's type.
  - An unpinned helper-token entry fails, a pin on an entry with a real-token first error fails, and a pin that names no corpus entry fails.

**Exempt:** message text, expecting-sets, and errors after the first.

**Pinned jolt constants,** one-line reasons in the harness. They all equal the JVM's now, because the patch gives the C++ helper the Java helper's coordinates.

**E1 self-test (7 PASS), so the checker is not vacuous.** Mutated jolt packets are refused for:
- an INDENT at line 0, which is the round-2 C++ helper's value: flagged `:validate!` and `:helper-pin-drift`;

- an empty message;
- no errors;
- an ok packet with one node changed.
