**(2) `reconfigure`** (interpreter.cljc:599-614, admission.cljc:296-304)

- Preserves everything it should: it touches only `[:source :admission]` and `:interpreter`. The link pair, the admission state (request, answer and question cursors, `:asked`, `:answers`, `:serial`) and `:context` are untouched. The interpreter keeps its question cursor. Test R asserts pair, admission, interpreter cursor and context equality and that the parked question is answered under the new decision on the next round.
- Decision-medium rule: equal handles keep the reader and its snapshot and ended flag (RingHandle is a deftype, so equality is identity, which is the right notion for "same medium"); a changed medium drops `:decision-cursor`, `:snapshot`, `:snapshot?` and `:ended?` and mints a cursor at the new medium's oldest end. Static to static works because `snapshot` reassigns the static value every round. Medium to static correctly discards a stale `:ended?`. The absent-snapshot and ended-snapshot rules of 7.5.8 therefore still hold after a reconfigure.
- External stays external: with no interpreter `after` is nil and no interpreter is attached (test E). Non-DHT is a documented no-op (E).
- Not retroactive: an appended allow is drained and retained after `reconfigure {}` (R sub-test), and the REPL-level case is U.
- No race: the REPL is value flow; `recheck-on-tick` and `drive-links` reassert `:source` from `:link-source` before each round, and `configure-admission` (repl.cljc:1815-1820) updates both the linker and `:link-source`, so a round can never read a stale policy.
