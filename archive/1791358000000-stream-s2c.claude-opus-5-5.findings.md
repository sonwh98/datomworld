## Edits

### `src/cljc/dao/stream/remote.cljc`

- **Policy.** `links` adds `:dao.stream.remote/give-up-after` to its policy and to the positive-integer-or-nil validation (`ex-info "invalid DaoStream remote link policy"`). `new-link` stores `:give-up-after` and `:now nil`.
- **Deadline stamping.** New `new-deadline` gives `now + give-up-after`, or nil when either is missing, so a clock-less composition stamps nothing. Following the sign-off note, the deadline is set where a request first exists on the link:
  - **Accepted sends.** `register!` now takes the deadline. `send-request!` and `refl-append` pass it on the writer's `ok`, and `send-named!` stamps it on `ok`.
  - **Kept probes.** `send-request!`'s keep branch stores `:deadline` on the `:pending` entry. A kept probe that is later sent, or refused again, carries its pending deadline: the lookup comes first, so it is never restamped.
  - **Waiting probes.** A probe waiting on its reflection (`:probe`, the S2b recovery path) has no deadline until `admit-probe!` gets it onto the link, where it is stamped at that moment.
  - **Resends.** `count-ask!` never touches `:deadline`.
  - **Answers.** An answer removes the outstanding entry, deadline included.
- **New `link-step!`, exposed as `links`' `:step`.** It is called as `(step cd now)`.
  - It records `:now`, then runs `drain!`, which is budgeted.
  - Unless the channel is already gone, it takes the least id among outstanding and pending entries with `:deadline <= now`. For that id it emits `{:dao.stream.remote/event :dao.stream.remote/channel-expired :dao.stream.remote/id id :dao.stream.remote/op op}`, then runs the existing `channel-loss!`. That abandons outstanding requests, emits `append-unknown` per abandoned append, and sets `channel-gone?`.
  - It answers `{:dao.stream.remote/channel-gone? bool :dao.stream.remote/expired id-or-nil}`, or nil for a channel this peer does not reach.
  - After the loss, the existing paths give `transport-error` / `channel-gone` (not retryable) for `cursor`, `next` and `resolve`, while `descriptor` still answers `ok`.
- Docstrings updated: `new-link`, `send-request!`, `links`, `attacher`.

### `src/cljc/dao/stream/ws_project.cljc`

- `dial`'s policy `select-keys` adds `:dao.stream.remote/give-up-after`, and the docstring says so. Only the namespaced key is accepted, as in spec §1.2; there is no bare `:give-up-after` alias.
- **`dial-step!` is now multi-arity.**
  - `([dial] (dial-step! dial nil))`: the 1-arity hands no `now`, so the link is not stepped and nothing ever expires. Existing callers are unchanged.
  - `([dial now])`:
    - It records `:now` on the dial, then runs the budgeted projection.
    - It then calls the link's `(:step! ch) (:cd ch) now` and runs the budgeted mirror.
    - If the step answers `channel-gone? true`, it closes the ws handle (idempotent). The host then deposits `:ws/closed`, and the projection closes the ring.
- **`establish!`** stores `:cd` and `:step!` in the channel map. When the dial has already been stepped with a `now`, it steps the new link at that `now` before the attach probe is sent.
  - Without this, the probe `dial-attach!` sends immediately would have no deadline, since the link didn't exist at the last step. The establishment stall the spec meant to bound would then go unbounded on the dial path.
  - A composition that never steps with `now` is unchanged.

## Tests

### `remote_test.cljc`

New fixtures: `stepped-links`, `attach-via`, `step-at`, `gone`.

- **`a-request-past-its-deadline-is-channel-loss`**
  - With give-up-after 10 and a step at 0, the probe, a `next` and an `append!` all carry deadline 10.
  - A step at 9 changes nothing.
  - A step at 10 answers gone with expired 0. The events are `channel-expired` (id 0, op descriptor), then `append-unknown` for the append.
  - Outstanding is then empty. `next`, `cursor` and `resolve` answer `channel-gone`; `descriptor` answers `ok`.
- **`a-resend-does-not-move-the-deadline`**
  - With resend-after 1, a `next` sent at 0 is re-sent across steps at 3, 6 and 9.
  - Its deadline stays at 10, and the step at 10 loses the channel.
- **`an-answer-before-the-deadline-clears-it`**: the probe and a `next` are answered and drained by step 12; a step at 20 finds no loss; the filed `:a` is returned.
- **`a-kept-probe-has-a-deadline`**
  - The writer always answers `full`. The kept probe has deadline 10, and a refused retry at step 5 keeps it.
  - The step at 10 expires id 0.
- **`without-a-stepped-now-nothing-expires`**: never stepped; nothing is stamped, the channel is not gone, and the reflection keeps re-sending.
- **`flood-cannot-defer-expiry`**: drain-budget 2 with 60 unrelated values queued; a step at 5 is not gone, and the step at 10 expires id 0.
- **`an-invalid-give-up-after-is-a-composition-error`**: 0, -1 and 1.5.
- **`a-step-of-an-unreached-channel-is-nil`.**
- **`drain-budget-counts-malformed-values-and-a-gap`** (S2b follow-up)
  - Setup: a 2-slot answer ring, so 3 values cause a gap, and drain-budget 2.
  - The 1st drain meets the gap. The 2nd reads the two malformed values left after the gap. The 3rd reads the probe's answer.

## Notes for review

1. `establish!` stepping the new link at the dial's last `now` is the one behavior beyond the brief's letter (rationale above). Without it, the `dial-step-with-now...` scenario only works if the first deadline-carrying request is sent after a later step.
2. `channel-loss!` still leaves `:pending` in place after expiry. The sign-off said this is inert: `drain!` returns early once the channel is gone, and `close!` clears it. I didn't change it.
