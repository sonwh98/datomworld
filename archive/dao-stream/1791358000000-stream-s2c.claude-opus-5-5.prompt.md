### Objective: Implement Slice S2c
Per the approved architectural specification in `collab/1791353752491-architect-stream-s2-spec.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md` §1.2, §2, §3, §4 and architect sign-off note (`collab/1791357800000-architect-stream-s2b-signoff.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md` §5):

1. **`src/cljc/dao/stream/remote.cljc`**:
   - Link policy validation: `:dao.stream.remote/give-up-after` (optional positive integer or nil).
   - Deadline stamping:
     - When `give-up-after` is set, `:deadline (+ now give-up-after)` is stamped on requests at their first accepted-or-kept send (inside `send!` accepting callers, and on kept probes when admitted/sent).
     - Probes waiting on their reflection have no deadline until admitted into the link.
     - Resends do NOT move the deadline (expiry stays at first send + `give-up-after`).
   - `:step` entry / driver-stepped `now`:
     - Driver supplies `now` via link step or reflection step. Without a stepped `now`, nothing expires.
     - When a request passes its deadline:
       - Emit `channel-expired` event on the event writer.
       - Trigger `channel-loss!`: outstanding requests abandoned, `append-unknown` for outstanding appends, `channel-gone?` becomes true.
       - Later `next`/`cursor`/`resolve` answer `transport-error` with reason `channel-gone` (not retryable).
       - `descriptor` still answers `ok`.
     - An answer before the deadline clears the request and deadline (no loss).
     - Flood does not defer expiry (drain budget bounds reads while clock advances).
   - Design docs: `docs/design/dao.stream.remote.md` §2.4 (new Expiry paragraph beside Channel loss), §2.5, §3.0. `docs/design/dao.stream.ws.md` (Deferred Liveness points to remote S2c).

2. **`src/cljc/dao/stream/ws_project.cljc`**:
   - `dial` accepts `:give-up-after` in link policy and config.
   - `dial-step!` 2-arity `(dial-step! dial now)`:
     - Steps the channel at `now`.
     - When channel expiry occurs, closes the ws handle; the projection sees `:ws/closed` and closes the ring; `dial-resolve!` answers `channel-gone`.
     - 1-arity `(dial-step! dial)` delegates without expiring (preserves backward compatibility).
