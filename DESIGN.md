# Design System: Datom.World (Taoist Monochrome Craft Edition)

## Visual World: "Taoist Ink & Void"
A return to the foundational Taoist aesthetic of datom.world: deep monochromatic ink, subtle misty mountain and bamboo silhouettes, pure white highlights, and algebraic tuple precision.

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Display & Headings**: `Cinzel` (ceremonial, timeless weight).
- **Body Prose**: `Spectral` (classical literary serif, 65–70ch measure, 1.75 line-height).
- **Tuples, Code & Specifications**: `JetBrains Mono` (tabular, precise, zero-cost semantic structure).
- **Prohibited**: Saturated AI gradients, colored side-tabs, generic Inter/Roboto faces.

## 2. Monochrome Taoist Palette
- **Void Canvas**: `#050608` deep ink core.
- **Atmospheric Mist Backdrop**: Fixed watermark SVG featuring mountain silhouettes, bamboo stalks, and the Yin-Yang medallion.
- **Surfaces**: `#0c0d11` (surface-1) and `#14151b` (surface-2).
- **Ink & Paper Contrast**:
  - Primary text & headlines: `#ffffff` pure white.
  - Secondary text: `#dcdcdc` light silver.
  - Muted text & metadata: `#959595`.
  - Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Buttons & Interactive Surfaces**:
  - Primary: Pure white surface (`#ffffff`) with black ink text (`#000000`).
  - Secondary: Translucent dark surface with silver border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`).

## 3. Surface & Structural Rules
- **No Card Clutter**: Flowing editorial sections and rhythmic axiom lists.
- **Tuples as Artifacts**: Monochromatic `[e a v t m]` mathematical notation.
- **Browser Micro-Surfaces**: Themed dark scrollbars, custom white selection tint, high-contrast focus rings.
