# Design System: Datom.World (Impeccable Craft Edition)

## Visual World: "Philosophic Monolith / Cyber-Daoist Stream"
A sublime union between Daoist natural philosophy (ink mist, bamboo, generative flow, balance of void and form) and deep theoretical computer science (monospaced formal specifications, algebraic tuple notation, execution traces).

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Editorial / Primary Sans**: `Newsreader` (or `Cinzel` / `Cabinet Grotesk` / `Outfit` / `Syne` / `Spectral` / `Fraunces` alternative):
  - Primary Body & Interface: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Syne` or `Outfit` or `Space Grotesk` -> **`Spectral`** (elegant, literary, philosophical serif) paired with **`JetBrains Mono`** / **`Fira Code`** for technical datom tuples and code, and **`Cabinet Grotesk`** / **`Cinzel Decorative`** for headlines.
  - Specifically:
    - Display / Headings: `Cinzel` & `Spectral` (Literary, timeless, ceremonial weight).
    - Technical Elements & Tuples: `JetBrains Mono` (tabular, precise, zero-cost semantic structure).
    - Body Text: `Spectral` (400/500, generous line-height 1.7, measure 65-72ch).
- **Prohibited**: Default generic Inter, Roboto, standard blue-purple AI gradients, generic nested cards.

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting
- **Atmosphere**: Deep Cosmic Obsidian Void (`#04060c` to `#080c16`) with misty organic depth.
- **Accents**:
  - Celestial Cyan / Ether: `#4ee1ff`
  - Mineral Jade / Bamboo: `#41e2ba`
  - Incense Amber / Gold: `#ffc15e`
  - Soft Lunar Pearl: `#e6edf8`
  - Daoist Mist: `rgba(240, 245, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(240, 245, 255, 0.12)`
  - Void Borders: `rgba(110, 140, 220, 0.18)`

## 3. Surface & Structural Rules
- **No Card Clutter**: Eliminate repetitive rectangular card grids. Use fluid typographic sections, rhythmic editorial columns, rule dividers, and structured data tables.
- **Tuples as First-Class Artifacts**: `[e a v t m]` rendered with mathematical reverence in dedicated tuple badges with subtle luminescent borders.
- **Interactive Micro-Craft**:
  - Precision focus rings (`outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 3px`).
  - Custom scrollbars tinted with deep jade/cyan.
  - Custom text selection: `background: rgba(78, 225, 255, 0.25); color: #ffffff;`.
