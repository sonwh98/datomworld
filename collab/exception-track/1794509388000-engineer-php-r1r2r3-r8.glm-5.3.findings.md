## Why option (a)

The kit never builds a nil condition — the anomaly can only arrive through a hostile or
broken caller-supplied raise exit (instrument level). Option (a) makes reception TOTAL
over that anomaly with ordinary local semantics (a documented TypeError, catchable like
any local error) instead of an operational door refusal that escapes the boundary
behavior; and no mint means no provenance is ever attached to a value the machinery
could not classify. This also keeps the r4 one-hop mint-reachability audit sound: the
new arm mentions only php/throw-error, no mint-surface name.
