## Why 14,087 assertions take longer than 93,396

* **Serial partitioning is established.** The shell’s fixed list left 29 PHP namespaces, 19 TS ANTLR namespaces, ten dao.star, six dao.host, six yang.call and four others (LaTTe, cross-language, runtime effects, TS callsite) in the same child. A listed namespace can have zero quick vars. The versioned registry already owns PHP, TS including the callsite, and dao.host. Applying its prefix rules to the recorded 74 separates 29 + 20 + 6 and leaves 19, including LaTTe. “Uncovered” is the name of the set difference. The shell scheduled it. [Recorded manifest](/home/sto/ci/nightly-results/20261123-0018-jvm-shards-dir/ns-uncovered.txt) · [Registry](../../src/dev/subsystems.edn)
* **An assertion does very different work in each shard.** The yang.base collection-order suite enumerates permutations, variants and constructors. A focused verification recorded 91,477 assertions in 15 tests on an earlier revision. In PHP quick, one aggregate test runs 518 generated programs (two variants × 259 IDs) before checking totals and mismatches. Another processed 271 PHPT entries, reporting 119 pass, 48 unsupported and 104 mismatches checked against the pinned manifest. Those 104 are corpus classifications. PHP slow evaluates 5,932 generated programs in the full permutation var alone and separately exercises every-VM fixtures and nine stress vars. Repeated lowering, projection, safepoint derivation, reconstruction and execution occur inside these loops. LaTTe’s differential gate logged 155 fixtures with separate observations and compilation/admission work. [Collection-order verification](../yang-clojure/1795109079000-engineer-fix-constructed-collection-order-r3.glm-5.3.findings.md) · [PHP corpus and assertions](../../test/yang/php/antlr/e2e_test.clj) · [PHP execution](../../test/yang/php/antlr/e2e_harness.clj) · [LaTTe gate](../../test/yang/latte_oracle_test.clj)

The peer’s observer recorded 257 s between the first and last of 102 consecutive LaTTe fixture completions. The completed isolated LaTTe run was 888.28 s, 37 tests/2,608 assertions green. PHP quick was deliberately stopped at 905.95 s, rc=143, without a verdict. Successive snapshots spanned at least 461 s in the same walker fixture test. These different-SHA measurements establish a costly test body without assigning it a percentage of the older shard. Real process launch, socket waits, polling and deliberate timeouts exist, especially in yin.repl and the dao.star loopback tests. The REPL/DHT process namespace is in yin.repl, outside this residual. The archived residual log has namespace banners without timestamps. There is no measured division of its 12,419 s among fixture setup, computation, children, sleep, GC and teardown. ## Orchestrator measurement and decision

1. **Bank the four dated lane receipts.** Preserve start/end times, expected/actual IDs, exits, skips and failures. Keep this incomplete/red pin as diagnostic evidence. Toggle a candidate on/off on one build or apply a documented harness-only overlay against the same base SHA. For an engine change, identify the code delta and compare both implementations against identical pinned test sources and fixtures.
2. **Scope the next probes.** These commands are for the orchestrator in a prepared disposable clone. The list operation plans without running tests. Instrument namespace/var begin/end and fixture phases in an already-required invocation where possible. Retain normal fixtures and terminal exits. A per-var timing harness that repeats a namespace’s once-fixture per var cannot reconstruct grouped wall.

3. **Accept one intervention at a time.** Compare paired baseline/candidate runs in alternating order, distinguishing cold dependency/build cache from fresh-process isolation. Repeat critical scoped pairs to estimate variability. For the full master matrix report both hosts’ starts/ends, prep/compiler/child/join times, last child, quick feedback wall, slow wall and elapsed time to the last required verdict.
