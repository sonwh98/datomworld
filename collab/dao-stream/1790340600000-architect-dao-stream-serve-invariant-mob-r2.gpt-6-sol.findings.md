**Q2 — CHANGE to GLM’s position.** Held reads should arrive with the meeting-convention milestone. I withdraw my claim that the retry budget adequately bounds polling: `:max-attempts` ends a session, while continued reachability through M requires the peer to start polling again. An interval bounds the *rate*, but trades it for inbound-call latency. A held demand reduces wire polling, though it still needs expiry and renewal; bound it to one demand per session, a hold duration, and finite work per M step. This need not gate serve-core loopback or direct channels. A cursor minted before the append helps find a published result, but absence after a reread cannot prove the request was not processed or that its announcement was not evicted. Give the request a stable ID; M deduplicates that ID and defines one active pair per peer ID, replaying the same descriptors for duplicates.
