**Q2 — option (b): `:serve/hold` is optional and additive in v1, enabled per deployment.** A meeting peer must work without it; neither peer is required to honor a hold request, and meeting acceptance must not depend on one. GLM is right that continuous reachability entails continuous polling cost: the retry cap ends an *unanswered* request, while an answered `blocked` demand is issued again. That establishes a traffic–latency tradeoff, not a correctness dependency on holds. The existing design already says a proxy tolerates an ignored hold flag. [GLM r2:10,38](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790340600000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-mob-r2.glm-5.3.findings.md:10), [original design:129-131,194](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790335900000-architect-dao-stream-serving-spec.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:129).

GLM’s edit 13 is **too ambiguous** about when a hold is available. Replace it with: “Inbound delivery through a meeting peer is demand-polled; polling cadence sets ongoing wire traffic and inbound-delivery latency. `:serve/hold` is an optional v1 optimization enabled per deployment, never a condition of meeting reachability or acceptance. A serving peer may ignore the flag and the proxy remains correct. A peer honoring it bounds held demands, duration, and work per step, and cancels holds on session loss.”

**Three corrections to GLM’s r2 list**

- **Idempotence — HOLD.** GLM retains rereading announcements before retrying. That is useful for finding a completed request, but a missing announcement does not prove the append failed: processing may be delayed or retention may have evicted the fact. `:meet/here` allocates a pair on each occurrence, while an unanswered append may already have taken effect. Require a stable request ID, M-side deduplication across sessions for the retry horizon, and one active pair per peer ID; a duplicate returns the same descriptors. [GLM r2:29](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790340600000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-mob-r2.glm-5.3.findings.md:29), [revision:149,153,158](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790339700000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-review.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:149), [original design:113-115](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790335900000-architect-dao-stream-serving-spec.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:113), [contract:674-679](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.md:674).
- **Privacy — HOLD.** GLM’s alternative of delivering an address through the called peer’s inbox does not itself provide privacy. The inbox descriptors are announced, and a descriptor grants no authorization. Keep an explicit public-address-disclosure statement; if confidentiality is intended, specify access control or encryption as an additional mechanism. [GLM r2:35](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790340600000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-mob-r2.glm-5.3.findings.md:35), [revision:51-53,153-158](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790339700000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-review.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:51), [contract:285-291](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.md:285).
- **Attribution — HOLD.** GLM is right that serve *frames* carry `:serve/session` and the serving side records a session attachment. It is wrong that both media carry the session ID: the appended `{:meet/here <peer-id>}` value carries neither session nor attachment, and the contract does not insert attachment metadata into appended values. Specify a serving-boundary observation that associates the accepted meeting append with its actual session and channel attachment; M must not derive `:meet/seen` from a peer-ID claim. [GLM r2:28](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790340600000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-mob-r2.glm-5.3.findings.md:28), [original design:51,68-70,158](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790335900000-architect-dao-stream-serving-spec.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:51), [revision:149-155](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/collab/1790339700000-architect-dao-stream-serve-invariant-review.claude-fable-5-1.findings.md:149), [contract:272-284](/Users/sto/workspace/datomworld/docs/design/dao.stream.md:272).
