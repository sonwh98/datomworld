`collab/p2probe-walk.clj` (a copy of the probe that also prints the walk

```text
rows: chain 6322  wide 5648  default-bounds {:max-parts 4096, :max-depth 256, :max-bytes 16777216}

  REFUSED ... :incomplete-closure ...
  WALK {:yin.link.closure/outcome :invalid, :role :row} {:code :parts-limit} :max-parts

  REFUSED ... :incomplete-closure ...
```

`publish-module!` (`publish.cljc` 189) calls `closure/walk` with no

either layout. Any receiver that fetches the closure under the default
bounds would refuse it in the same way (section 4.2 step 2).

The chain layout may also exceed `:max-depth 256`, because the `then`
chain nests about 340 deep in the row graph. This was not measured: an
attempt to print that walk under a raised `:max-parts` stalled for more
than 10 minutes and was abandoned. The chain layout is not the design's
choice, so the question was not pursued.

So 350 of 360 samples are in `tree-free-name-occurrences`

## 3. What this means for P2

A1, A3, A5, A6 and A7, and every linked e2e and c3 leg, publish `py`
and/or link it. Under today's code each of those either is refused by F1
or, with F1 lifted, costs about 46 minutes per publish on the JVM (F2).

are resolved. The prelude-side work (D4 renames, D7, D8, D6 placeholders,

independent of F1 and F2 and could proceed, but section 5 says to stop,
so none of it was started.

Decisions for the Architect (listed, not chosen):

- F1, bounds. For example: `publish-module!` and `link-local` take caller
  bounds; the default `:max-parts` is raised to cover a module of this
  size; or the `py` tree is made smaller (several modules, or shared
  subtrees). The first two are linker-seat changes section 5 prohibits
  here. The third changes the module layout, which section 5 also
  prohibits.
- F2, scanner cost. `tree-free-name-occurrences` (and possibly the other
  tree-format scanners built on the same relations) needs a cheaper
  derivation for a tree of thousands of rows, for example a direct
  recursive walk of the rows instead of the Datalog rules. Section 5
  prohibits scanner changes in this slice.
- Whether the receiving side's link of `py` pays the F2 cost per task.
  This needs one measurement before the linked e2e legs are designed.
