## Probe and observations

`test/yang/correspondence_launch_probe.py` (SHA-256 `b8a6d3f067df38508d7c29e22fc3433d82317bd6bb4feb24b0a94b46338396b7`) obtains the local `clojure -Spath -M:test` classpath, requires 154 entries, and removes indices 5–7: the Python, PHP and TypeScript generated parser class directories. It creates temporary copies of the jars at indices 76 and 77, excluding exactly `META-INF/native/libnetty_transport_native_epoll_x86_64.so` and `META-INF/native/libnetty_transport_native_kqueue_x86_64.jnilib`, and refuses unexpected native-entry names. The other classpath entries and their order are preserved. The copies are test instrumentation, not proposed retained dependency bytes.

The script starts `/usr/bin/java` with `-Xss64m`, `-Dfile.encoding=UTF-8`, English/US language properties, and a deliberately small environment (`PATH`, temporary `HOME` and `TMPDIR`, `LANG=C`, `LC_ALL=C`). On this machine that launcher reported OpenJDK 21.0.2. The working directory remains the invoking checkout; classpath entries still point to the checkout and local dependency caches, and the installed Java and host remain available. It invokes only `test-d1-amended-replay-closed-store`, whose own assertions check one `:beta-eq-false` fresh admission, compilation, stamping, retained-input readback and producer-side reconstruction. The probe requires exactly one test and six passing assertions. It is reproducible locally with `python3 test/yang/correspondence_launch_probe.py` when the expected local classpath layout exists; the `clojure -Spath` setup step itself is not environment-isolated.

Two runs passed 1 test / 6 assertions each, with no failures or errors. The first removed the directories and native entries; the second, represented by the current script, also redefined `yin.vm.macro/expand` and `expand-batch` to throw during the test. The namespace was loaded before those redefinitions. The result shows that neither of these entrypoints was called during this tested compilation/replay. It does not exclude other expansion paths or prove behavior during namespace initialization, for other fixtures, or for the historical admission. The direct composition calls `d1-assemble`, `yang/compile-program` with `true`, and `kernel-stamp-sites`, while the frontend's namespace documentation distinguishes its rewrites from row-native macro lookup. Together these are useful evidence for the candidate bypass, not the ruling's package-pinned checked execution and complete applicability declaration.

For this one path, the three observed generated parser directories and those two jar-embedded native binaries were not required while absent from the declared classpath. This does not establish whether a future reconstruction entrypoint, its full fixture/adaptation coverage, initialization, or another supported host needs them. The test does not intercept all filesystem, dynamic-library or resource accesses, and does not prove the absence of other undeclared dependencies. It therefore would be premature to exclude these inputs from a claimed complete plan or to call all native-library necessity resolved.
