## What was captured

Added `src/cljc/yang/correspondence_inventory.py`, a standalone observational capture helper. It archives each entry of an explicitly supplied resolved classpath in order, and the supplied runtime directory, into individual SHA-256-addressed tar files. Directory archives include the contents present at capture time, including uncommitted/generated files, modes and symlinks; regular classpath files are archived as files. It records resolved absolute source paths as descriptive observations, kinds, archive sizes and hashes. Capture checks existing addressed objects; `verify` reopens and hashes every referenced archive. It does not extract archives, provide safe extraction, or establish a closed executable dependency set. Absolute and escaping symlinks can be present. Original top-level symlink spellings are resolved before capture.

In a separate Python process, all referenced sizes and hashes were verified after the capture process exited.
