- `retain-packet-call-provenance!` now tags every throw with its stage via `retention-throw`: `:validation` (provenance validation refusal), `:relation` (relation `materialize!`), `:record` (record `materialize!`), plus admission and root. Its throwing contract is otherwise unchanged.
