## Why the requested run and publications remain open

Design §4.2 requires a retrievable D1 record and recipe, image contract and derivation checks, independent reconstruction and instruction mapping, complete scoped lanes, and a separate result. The scratch outcomes retain six segment addresses per admission, not the D1 record/recipe or a pinned executable and expansion dependency closure. `check-fixture!` expressly excludes admission authenticity, executable-image derivations and trace completeness; `attributed?` assumes pre-scoped complete lane traces and trusts the supplied `:complete?` flag. The runner `test/yang/clojure_test.clj` `run-guest-machine` discards the successful instruction counter, so it cannot report actual guest instructions as required. The r9 and r7 findings describe the still-missing provenance/image checks. Row-lane-only remains the stated restriction.

Design §6.3 also requires row/occurrence/path, static storage and memory, trace capacity in records and bytes with loss/drain accounting, guest work and baselines, per-phase time, and peak memory ceilings, all before the tagged run. r9b did not retain heap samples or per-phase timing, and lane/baseline measurements were not recorded. The historical cc3 4,000,000 instructions and 1,000,000 records are baseline settings, not measurements of this run, and do not establish byte capacity or completeness. A pre-run declaration cannot honestly be constructed from those missing values. No tagged lane was run, and neither `slice9_verification.edn` nor `slice9_correspondence.edn` was fabricated. Implement and red-test bounded counters, drain/loss/completeness refusal and the remaining seven-step verifier; measure appropriate preflight workloads; freeze a finite max-based declaration; only then execute and publish the actual result, including any exceedance as failure.

The two specifically named architect ruling files were not present in this worktree under the supplied names; r5 records the numeric fidelity amendment, but that secondary account is not a substitute for the normative originals. Obtain the original rulings before asserting their full compliance.
