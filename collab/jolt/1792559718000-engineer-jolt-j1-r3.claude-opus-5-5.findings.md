## 1. J1-R4: float64-of and float-value on decimal and ratio carriers

Added to `src/dev/jolt_profile_compare.cljc`. Each input is run through both `number/float64-of` and `number/float-value`:

- **Decimals (16 inputs × 2 = 32 cases):** 1.5, -1.5, 0, 0E-5, 0.1, 123E7, 1E400 (+inf), -1E400 (-inf), 1E-400 (0.0), 2^53+1 (tie → 2^53), 2^53+3 (tie → 2^53+4), 2^-53 exactly, 1+2^-53 (tie → 1.0), 1+3·2^-53 (tie → 1+2^-51), 1+2^-53+1E-60 (just above the tie), a 40-digit value.
  - **Result: identical on both hosts, all 32.** jolt's `(double <BigDecimal record>)` and the JDK's `BigDecimal.doubleValue` are both correctly rounded.
- **Ratios (12 inputs × 2 = 24 cases):** 1/3, -1/3, 2/3, 0/5 and 7/1 (Rational carrier for denominator 1), 10^400/3 (+inf), -10^400/7 (-inf), 1/10^400 (0.0), (2^53+1)/2^53 (tie → 1.0), (2^53+3)/2^53 (tie → 1+2^-51), a value just above the 1+2^-53 tie, (2^54+1)/2.
  - **9 inputs are identical**, including the Rational-carrier pair: on both hosts `(double <Rational deftype>)` throws a plain exception with no ex-data, so the case reads `[:threw []]`.
  - **3 inputs differ:**

| Ratio | Exact value | Nearest binary64 (ties to even) | JVM `(double r)` | jolt | `float/ratio->float` (JVM and jolt) |
|-------|-------------|----------------------------------|------------------|------|------------------------------------|
| 2/3 | 0.666… | 3fe5555555555555 | 3fe5555555555556 | 3fe5555555555555 | 3fe5555555555555 |
| (2^53+3)/2^53 | 1 + 2^-52 + 2^-53 (a tie) | 3ff0000000000002 | 3ff0000000000000 (2 ulps off) | 3ff0000000000002 | 3ff0000000000002 |
| just above the 1+2^-53 tie | 1+2^-53+10^-60 | 3ff0000000000001 | 3ff0000000000000 | 3ff0000000000001 | 3ff0000000000001 |

- **Why the JVM is wrong.** Clojure's `clojure.lang.Ratio.doubleValue` is `decimalValue(MathContext.DECIMAL64).doubleValue()`. It rounds to 16 significant decimal digits first and then to binary64, i.e. two roundings.
  - The profile's law for `float64-of` is "floating-point content with the value of host number `x`".
  - The profile's own correctly-rounded kernel answers the same as jolt **on the JVM itself**: the three `float/ratio->float reference ...` cases are identical on both hosts.
- **Decision.** No change to `src/jolt/dao/host/number.jolt`: the difference is outside the jolt override.
  - The cases carry a `KNOWN DIFFERENCE` comment in the compare script.
  - Recommendation for the host-boundary seat: the JVM arm of `float64-of`/`float-value` on a `clojure.lang.Ratio` should go through `dao.host.float/ratio->float` (or `BigDecimal` division at full precision) rather than `(double r)`. That is a shared-file change, outside this track's rules.
  - What JS and Dart answer for ratio content is not covered here.

## 2. J1-R5: units->string on a unit outside 0..0xFFFF

- **JVM shape (observed with `clojure -M -e ...` on the shared `dao.host.text`).** Each of `[65536]`, `[-1]`, `[97 1114112]` and `[55296 65536]` throws `java.lang.IllegalArgumentException`, message `"Value out of range for char: <u>"`, ex-data nil. There is no `:dao.host/refusal`; it is the host's `(char u)`. The JVM reaches it even after an unpaired surrogate, which it can hold.
- **Reproducible exactly on jolt:** `(IllegalArgumentException. msg)` is a jolt shim class, `instance?` answers true, and ex-data is nil.
- **Change** (`src/jolt/dao/host/text.jolt`, `units->string`). Before anything else, every integer unit is checked in order. The first one outside 0..0xFFFF throws that exception with that message. The docstring states the case.
- **Compare cases added** (`text/units->string range ...`). Each records `[:threw (instance? IllegalArgumentException e) (ex-message e) (ex-data e)]` or the result. Inputs: `[0xFFFF]`, `[0]`, `[65536]`, `[-1]`, `[97 1114112]`, `[55296 65536]`, `[56320 97 70000]`.
  - All 5 throwing cases are identical on both hosts: class, message, ex-data nil.
  - The 2 in-range cases differ only by carrier tag (the JVM's `Integer` from `code-unit`, which is the known carrier class).
- **Not changed:** non-integer or nil units (the JVM truncates `1.5` with `(char 1.5)` and throws NPE on nil). They are outside the declared domain, and the brief scoped R5 to units above 0xFFFF.
